BRANDING & DIGITAL MARKETING

Coffee Hub

Developed a digital marketing concept for a coffee brand focused on building a strong local identity and drive online customer engagement.

Brand Strategy Content Marketing Social Media
View Case Study

Project Overview

Coffee Hub is a concept for a neighbourhood coffee shop in Harare. The marketing work is intended to give the business a recognisable local identity and help nearby people discover it, choose it for a visit and return.

The Challenge

A new or growing café needs to stand out among local options and turn online attention into in-person visits. Its communication also needs to give people a reason to come back, not just notice the brand once.

My Role

I developed the digital marketing concept, with a focus on brand positioning, content themes, social media engagement and promotional ideas. The project materials do not specify particular analytics or advertising tools.

Strategy and Execution

Position Coffee Hub as a welcoming, convenient local coffee stop. Prioritise Harare residents, nearby workers and students, and people looking for a place to meet. Build discoverability through a complete local business profile, consistent contact details, location-led page copy and useful search phrases such as “coffee shop in Harare” and relevant neighbourhood terms. Use social content to show menu items, the café experience and timely offers; connect promotions to clear visit prompts and encourage customers to share feedback and return through recurring offers. These are proposed tactics, not reported campaign results.

Tools Used

Specific tools used were not recorded in the available project details.

Expected Outcomes

The strategy is designed to improve local awareness and discovery, encourage first visits, create more social interaction and support repeat visits. No measured outcomes are available.

Key Learning

This concept demonstrates how local SEO, useful social content and in-store promotions can work together around one practical goal: making a nearby café easier to find and worth revisiting.

SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMISATION

SEO Audit

An audit project focused on identifying search visibility and website optimisation opportunities, then organising them into practical recommendations.

Keyword Research On-page SEO Technical Review
View Case Study

Project Overview

This SEO audit reviews a website's search-readiness and identifies opportunities to make its pages clearer to search engines and more useful to visitors. The available project material does not identify a client or publish audit findings.

The Challenge

Websites can miss relevant organic searches when page topics, content structure, technical accessibility or user experience do not support what people are looking for. An audit helps prioritise these issues before making changes.

My Role

I approached the work as an SEO review: considering search intent, page relevance, technical factors and content opportunities, then translating the review into recommendations. Specific tools and verified site-level findings were not recorded in the available details.

Strategy and Execution

Start with keyword and intent research, then map relevant themes to useful pages rather than targeting terms in isolation. Review titles, headings, page copy, internal links and calls to action for relevance and clarity. Check technical considerations such as crawlability, indexation, mobile usability, page speed and broken links. Compare competitor page topics and formats to identify content gaps, then prioritise recommendations by likely value and effort. These are audit areas and recommendations; no specific fixes or ranking changes are claimed.

Tools Used

Specific SEO tools used were not recorded in the available project details.

Expected Outcomes

Applying prioritised recommendations is intended to improve technical accessibility, page relevance and the site's potential to earn qualified organic visits. No traffic, ranking or performance improvements have been measured or supplied.

Key Learning

The project demonstrates a structured approach to SEO: connect search intent and useful content with sound on-page and technical foundations, then make recommendations that can be prioritised and evaluated.

MARKETING STRATEGY & TEAM PROJECT

Karukuzi Safety Wear

As Marketing Lead and Strategist on a team project, I researched the target market, developed customer personas and coordinated campaign planning.

Marketing Strategy Market Research Campaign Planning Team Leadership
View Case Study

Project Overview

Karukuzi Safety Wear focuses on protective workwear for construction workers. The team project developed a marketing strategy to clarify the audience, strengthen the brand's position and create a practical route to customer enquiries.

The Challenge

Buyers need safety wear that meets the demands of job sites, is dependable and can be sourced with confidence. The business needs to communicate its value to both people who wear the products and those responsible for purchasing them.

My Role

I served as Marketing Lead and Strategist in a team project. My documented responsibilities included researching the target market, defining customer personas, developing the marketing strategy, planning campaigns and coordinating the team's marketing activities.

Strategy and Execution

Focus on construction workers as users and site managers, contractors and procurement decision-makers as potential buyers. Research competing suppliers and compare their product range, positioning and communication before defining Karukuzi's differentiators. Build content around product use, worker protection, product selection and enquiry prompts. Plan awareness and lead-generation activity across appropriate digital channels, with campaign objectives linked to qualified enquiries. Set KPIs such as reach, engagement, enquiry volume and lead quality before launch; no campaign performance is claimed here.

Tools Used

Specific research, publishing or advertising tools were not recorded in the available project details.

Expected Outcomes

The strategy is intended to improve brand understanding among relevant buyers, generate qualified product enquiries and give the team measurable campaign goals. No sales or lead results are available.

Key Learning

This project shows how audience research, competitor review, clear positioning and coordinated team planning can turn a broad marketing need into an actionable lead-generation direction.

04

DIGITAL ADVERTISING

Digital Advertising Campaign

Developed campaign concepts connecting objectives, audience selection, ad messaging, creative formats and calls to action.

Google Ads Meta Ads Campaign Strategy
View Case Study

Project Overview

This advertising project develops a campaign plan from its objective through to its creative message and evaluation. The available project material describes campaign concepts, not a live campaign or measured client results.

The Challenge

Paid activity needs a clear purpose and a message that is relevant to the intended audience. Without alignment between audience, platform, creative and next step, spend can generate attention without useful action.

My Role

I created advertising concepts covering campaign objectives, audiences, ad formats, messaging and calls to action. The project materials identify Google Ads and Meta Ads as relevant platforms but do not specify a launched campaign or results.

Strategy and Execution

Begin by defining one primary objective, such as awareness, traffic or lead generation, and match it to an audience and destination. Use Google Ads when the plan is built around active search intent; use Meta Ads for audience-led discovery and visual storytelling. Adapt the creative to the placement, with concise copy, a clear benefit and a direct call to action. Where budget and traffic allow, test one variable at a time, such as headline, image or CTA, and keep the audience and objective consistent for a useful comparison.

Tools and Platforms

Google Ads and Meta Ads are identified in the project materials as campaign platforms. The materials do not confirm that ads were launched through either platform.

Evaluation and Expected Outcomes

Evaluate against the chosen objective: reach and frequency for awareness, click-through rate and landing-page engagement for traffic, or conversion rate and cost per qualified lead for lead generation. A/B testing is a proposed evaluation method. The intended outcome is more relevant reach and useful customer action; there are no reported performance results.

Key Learning

This work demonstrates the importance of treating advertising as a connected system: objective, audience, placement, message, creative and measurement should all support the same action.
